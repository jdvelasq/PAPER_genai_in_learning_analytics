# Research Objective and Research Questions

## General Objective

To systematically map the scientific literature on the use and impact of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) and Agentic Artificial Intelligence (Agentic AI) in Learning Analytics (LA) and Teaching Analytics (TA), identifying research themes, technological functions, applications, methodological approaches, empirical evidence, limitations, and emerging research gaps.

## Research Questions

**RQ1 — Research themes and evolution.** What are the dominant and emerging research themes concerning GenAI and Agentic AI in LA and TA, and how have they evolved over time?

**RQ2 — Technological functions.** What functions do generative and agentic AI technologies perform in LA and TA, and what evidence supports their operational differentiation?

**RQ3 — Objects and stakeholders.** Which learning and teaching data, processes, decisions, and interventions are addressed by these technologies, and which stakeholders benefit from them?

**RQ4 — Research methods and validation.** What research designs, educational contexts, evaluation metrics, and types of validation are reported in the studies, and what limitations are identified?
